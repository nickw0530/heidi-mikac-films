A few helpful details

Before you
check out.

How does merch shipping work?

$5 U.S. shipping per order, even with multiple items. Your selected shirt size and each item appear in your cart before you pay. For order questions, email hello@heidimikacfilms.com.

Can I use PayPal, Venmo, or a card?

Yes. Add your merch to the cart and continue to checkout to see the payment options available to you. Venmo appears for eligible buyers through PayPal Checkout.

Can I contribute without buying anything?

Absolutely. Use Make a contribution to choose your amount on Crowdfundr. Contributions and campaign perks are checked out there; this merch cart is for purchases directly from Heidi Mikac Films.

Will my name or amount be public?

You choose separately whether to share your name and merch support amount at checkout. Both are private by default. Support without a public name appears as Anonymous. Your email and address never appear on the wall. To change your preferences later, email hello@heidimikacfilms.com. Crowdfundr privacy choices are respected when contributions are imported; imports are not a live connection.
