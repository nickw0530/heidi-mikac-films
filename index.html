Behind the stories

Heidi Mikac.

Filmmaker, writer, director, producer, and performer.

Heidi Mikac creates intimate, character-driven films about people under pressure — grief, control, identity, body image, secrecy, and the moments when private pain becomes impossible to hide.

Her work moves between drama and psychological suspense without losing sight of the human being at the center of the story.
