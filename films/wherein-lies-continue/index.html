Wherein Lies Continue

Suspense thriller•In production•Coming soon
A Heidi Mikac Films production

Every town has secrets.

When a series of mysterious disappearances grips the quiet town of Lemon Creek, investigative journalist Melanie becomes determined to uncover the truth.

The deeper she digs, the more disturbing the mystery becomes. Buried secrets begin to surface, familiar faces become suspects, and the line between trust and deception starts to disappear.

As Melanie closes in on the truth, she discovers that some secrets are closer to home than she ever imagined — and uncovering them could cost her everything.
